### Hey, I'm Aloïs Heloir 👋

**Senior Product Engineer** at PflegeNavi · 📍 Vienna, Austria

I've spent nearly 5 years shipping production software in healthcare, e-commerce and security. I own products end to end, from architecture and delivery to deployment and the first paying users. I usually build with **Vue / Nuxt** on the frontend and **TypeScript, Elixir or Rust** on the backend.

- 🏥 Right now I'm building healthcare fintech at PflegeNavi
- 🛒 Before that I was Lead Developer at Ringana, leading 5 engineers on a €300M e-commerce platform
- 🤖 I'm currently exploring AI engineering: LLM tool calling, agents, dev tooling and software factories
- 🎓 MSc in Computer Science (Diplôme d'Ingénieur) from EPITA, Paris
- 🗣️ French (native) · English (fluent)
